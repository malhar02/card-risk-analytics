HOW TO READ THE METRICS

Ranking quality and workload

PR AUC

Precision-recall area summarizes ranking behavior with an emphasis on the positive class. It is not a business impact estimate.

Precision

Among accounts or transactions sent to review, the share carrying the positive benchmark label.

Recall / capture

Among positive benchmark labels, the share routed to review at the chosen threshold.

Review rate

Share of the evaluated sample flagged for manual review. Capacity and label quality matter.

BEFORE ANY REAL-WORLD USE

What this project cannot establish

  • Neither benchmark contains American Express data or reflects current Amex performance.
  • The fraud data lack customer, merchant, device, network, and operational context; their positive rate is not a live portfolio prevalence.
  • The credit sample is a historical Taiwan snapshot with a random test split, not a test of present-day or temporal generalization.
  • Model scores are not calibrated probabilities. Observed rates by segment are benchmark outcomes, not forecasts for a current population.
  • Review flags are analytical examples only. They do not recommend, automate, or authorize payment blocking, account approval, or credit decisions.
