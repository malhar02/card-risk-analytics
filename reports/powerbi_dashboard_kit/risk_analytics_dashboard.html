Decision context

Fraud: 89 fraud transactions were flagged, with 224 legitimate transactions also sent to review. Precision and recall describe this benchmark and threshold only.

Credit: The model excludes sex, education, marital status, and age. Its hypothetical review queue only demonstrates ranking and workload trade-offs; it is not an approval or decline recommendation.

Data limits: ULB fraud records are anonymized and span a short historical period. The UCI default benchmark represents a historical Taiwan bank snapshot. Neither dataset represents American Express customers, operating data, or current U.S. credit policy.
